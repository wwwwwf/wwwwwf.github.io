About Me

I am a master’s student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Xiaofeng Hou and Prof. Chao Li. I am a member of the Sustainable Architecture and Intelligence Lab (SAIL).

My research focuses on computer architecture and systems for efficient AI, particularly large-model inference under limited computing and memory resources.

I study how model execution, memory management, and data movement can be coordinated to improve inference efficiency. My recent work explores mixture-of-experts (MoE) inference, speculative offloading, and KV-cache reuse for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

I welcome discussions and research collaborations on efficient AI systems. Feel free to reach me by email.

Research Interests

  • Efficient LLM inference: MoE serving, speculative execution, and model quantization.
  • Memory and storage systems: expert offloading, KV-cache reuse, and prefetching.
  • Architecture–system co-design: coordinating computation and communication on resource-constrained platforms.
